Best Walks Nearby

Fowey sits on the South West Coast Path — some of the most spectacular walking in England is right on your doorstep.

Fowey to Polkerris — Coastal Path

4.5 miles Moderate South West Coast Path

Head west along the coast path from Readymoney Cove, through dramatic cliff scenery to the lovely little harbour at Polkerris. Grab a pasty at Sam's on the Beach and catch the bus back or walk back the same way.

Hall Walk — Fowey Estuary Circular

4 miles circular Easy–Moderate Family-friendly

A classic Fowey walk. Take the passenger ferry from Town Quay to Polruan, walk up through the woods along Hall Walk with stunning estuary views, descend to Bodinnick, and take the car ferry back. Absolutely beautiful at any time of year.

Readymoney Cove & St Catherine's Castle

1.5 miles Easy Historic

A gentle stroll from the town centre down to the pretty sandy cove at Readymoney, then up to the Tudor fort at St Catherine's Point for panoramic views of the harbour mouth. Perfect for an evening walk.

Fowey to Lostwithiel — River Walk

6 miles one way Easy River estuary

Follow the Fowey river upstream through tidal creeks and ancient woodland to the historic town of Lostwithiel. A wonderfully peaceful walk away from the crowds. Train back from Lostwithiel to Par, then taxi or bus to Fowey.
