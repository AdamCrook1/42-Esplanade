Best Walks Nearby

Fowey sits on the South West Coast Path — some of the most spectacular walking in England is right on your doorstep.

Fowey to Polkerris — Coastal Path

4.5 milesModerate

Head west along the coast path from Readymoney Cove through dramatic cliff scenery to the lovely harbour at Polkerris. Grab a pasty at Sam's on the Beach.

Hall Walk — Fowey Estuary Circular

4 miles circularEasy–ModerateFamily-friendly

Take the passenger ferry to Polruan, walk through the woods along Hall Walk with stunning estuary views, descend to Bodinnick, and take the car ferry back.

Readymoney Cove & St Catherine's Castle

1.5 milesEasy

A gentle stroll down to the pretty sandy cove at Readymoney, then up to the Tudor fort for panoramic views of the harbour mouth.

Fowey to Lostwithiel — River Walk

6 miles one wayEasy

Follow the Fowey river upstream through tidal creeks and ancient woodland to historic Lostwithiel. Train back from Lostwithiel to Par.

Best Restaurants

Fowey has a brilliant food scene for a small town. Book ahead in summer — it gets very busy.

Narla ↗

Fine DiningMichelin RecommendedBook Ahead

One of Fowey's most exciting restaurants — modern Cornish cooking from Charlie Walters. Michelin recommended. Book well in advance.

Sam's Fowey ↗

Lunch & DinnerSeafoodLively

A Fowey institution since 1987 — brilliant burgers, locally landed fish, lobster and mussels from their own day boat. Great cocktail bar too.

Pintxo ↗

TapasWalk-ins OnlyEsplanade

Lovely Spanish tapas and sherry bar on the Esplanade. No bookings needed — just turn up. Perfect for a relaxed evening.

Harbour Hotel — Harbour Kitchen ↗

DinnerViewsSmart Casual

Stunning harbour views from the terrace. Local seafood, steaks and seasonal dishes. Great for a special occasion dinner.

The King of Prussia ↗

PubTown QuayBritish

Historic pub right on Town Quay — proper British food done brilliantly, with great ales and lovely estuary views. A Fowey classic.

Havener's Bar & Grill ↗

Bar & GrillWaterfrontSt Austell Ales

Waterside bar and grill on Town Quay with one of the best views in Fowey. Seasonal pub classics, local seafood and excellent St Austell ales.

Samphire ↗

Fine DiningWaterfrontSeasonal

Relaxed coastal fine dining at The Old Quay House. Panoramic water views and a beautifully seasonal menu. Book ahead.

Sunny Spice ↗

IndianSouth IndianFore Street

Wonderful South Indian food on Fore Street — a real local favourite. Contemporary Indian cooking with fresh Cornish ingredients.

Sam's on the Beach, Polkerris ↗

Lunch & DinnerSeafoodBeach

Right on the beach at Polkerris in a restored RNLI lifeboat house — amazing seafood, wood-fired pizza and brilliant burgers. Walk there via the coast path for bonus points.

The Crown Inn, Lanlivery ↗

Pub10 mins driveCornish

One of Cornwall's oldest and most loved pubs — a gorgeous stone inn in the village of Lanlivery. Cracking Cornish food and real ales. Well worth the trip.
